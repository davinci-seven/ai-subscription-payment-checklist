# ChatGPT Plus Payment Checklist

Use this before trying another card for ChatGPT Plus.

## Check billing route

- If subscribed through ChatGPT web checkout, update billing in ChatGPT/OpenAI.
- If subscribed through Apple, update Apple payment method.
- If subscribed through Google Play, update Google payment method.

## Check card and issuer

- Card is active.
- Balance covers subscription plus tax or authorization buffer.
- International online payments are enabled.
- Recurring payments are enabled.
- Bank app has no security block.

## Check details

- Billing name is accurate.
- Address and postal code are consistent.
- CVV and expiry are correct.
- 3D Secure or other authentication can complete.

## If testing a virtual card

- Start with a small balance.
- Avoid repeated failed retries.
- Track the renewal date.
- Confirm renewal before relying on the card long term.
