# Claude Pro Payment Checklist

Use this before trying another card for Claude Pro.

## Basic checks

- Confirm Claude Pro is available for your account and region.
- Check whether the card supports international recurring subscriptions.
- Check balance, limits and bank security blocks.
- Keep billing details accurate and consistent.

## Virtual card checks

- Verify the card is Visa, Mastercard or another accepted network.
- Confirm funding fees, card fees and withdrawal/refund path.
- Use a small balance first.
- Track renewal behavior.
